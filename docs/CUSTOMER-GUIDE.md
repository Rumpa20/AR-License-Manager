# AR License Manager v1.0 — Customer Guide

(For customers of ARNIT CREATION digital products.)

Copyright (c) ARNIT CREATION.

## Getting your license

When you buy a product on the ARNIT CREATION store, a license key is
generated automatically after payment is confirmed and sent to the email
address used at checkout. It looks like:

```
ARLM-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
```

Keep it safe. You will need it to activate the product on your site.

## Activating your license

1. Open the product's settings page (e.g. your forum admin → product's
   license setting, or the software's activation screen).
2. Paste the license key and confirm. The software sends your domain to the
   ARNIT license server over HTTPS and records the activation.
3. If your activation limit is reached, either deactivate an old domain
   first (see below) or contact support.

## Managing licenses in the SMF member portal

If your account is on an ARNIT CREATION forum, log in and open
**My Licenses** (`index.php?action=licenses`):

- Every license linked to your account email is listed with its product,
  status badge and expiry date. Licenses are matched by the email on your
  forum account - if a purchase is missing, check that your store email
  address matches this account, then contact support.
- License details show the product information, activation status, the
  domains activated so far, and how many activation slots remain.
- **Deactivate Domain**: freeing a slot on a domain you no longer use (e.g.
  an old site) lets you activate a new one. You can only deactivate domains
  on your own licenses.
- Activation history is available for support disputes.

## What each status means

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Active | Everything is fine | Nothing |
| Expired | Past expiry date | Renew, or contact sales |
| Suspended | Temporarily disabled, usually pending a payment or investigation | Contact support |
| Revoked | Permanently disabled (chargeback, fraud, final action) | Contact support only if you believe it is an error |
| Pending | Created but not yet approved/paid | Complete payment or wait for approval |

## Support requests

Open a support topic in the forum's support board, or reply to your delivery
email. Always include the **order reference** from your store email; support
never needs your license key itself and support staff cannot read stored
keys (they are kept hashed, not plaintext).

## Privacy

Only the following is stored: your email address, your name (if provided at
checkout), your forum member id (if the purchase is linked to a forum
account), and your activation domains. No passwords, payment card data or
browsing data is collected by the license system.
